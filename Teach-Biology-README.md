# Teach Biology Chat Mode

> **Disclaimer:** The content generated by AI may not always be accurate or appropriate. Please review and verify all information before using it for teaching or learning purposes.

The **Teach Biology** chat mode turns GitHub Copilot into an interactive biology teacher bot. It helps users learn biological concepts, processes, and laboratory techniques through safe, adaptive, and engaging instruction, prioritizing safety above all else.

## Key Features
- **Adaptive Explanations**: Adjusts to your biology knowledge level
- **Quiz Mode**: Interactive questions to test your understanding of biology topics
- **Laboratory Mode**: Step-by-step guidance for understanding safe biological experiments and observations
- **Safety-First Approach**: Emphasizes theoretical understanding and safe, supervised activities only
- **Web Search Integration**: Access to up-to-date biology resources and educational content

## How It Works
- Ask about biological concepts, processes, or laboratory techniques
- Request explanations with biological examples and analogies
- Try quiz mode to test your biology knowledge
- Explore laboratory procedures through theoretical understanding and safety considerations
- Get personalized instruction adapted to your knowledge level

## Topics Covered
- Basic biology fundamentals (cells, tissues, organs, organ systems)
- Cell structure and function
- Genetics and heredity
- Evolution and natural selection
- Ecology and ecosystems
- Physiology (plant and animal)
- Microbiology and classification of life
- Human biology and health
- Molecular biology and biochemistry basics
- Laboratory safety and procedures
- Experimental design and data analysis

## Critical Safety Protocols
**This mode will NEVER provide instructions for:**
- Experiments involving dangerous organisms, pathogens, or biohazards
- Genetic engineering or modification without proper supervision
- Procedures that could cause injury, infection, or environmental harm
- Experiments that should only be conducted in professional laboratory or clinical settings

**Instead, the focus is on:**
- Theoretical understanding of biological concepts
- Safe experiments using common household materials
- Proper educational resources and supervised learning environments
- Laboratory safety principles and ethical practices

## Example Prompts
- "What are the main parts of a plant cell and their functions?"
- "Explain the process of photosynthesis for a middle school student."
- "Quiz me on genetics basics with 3 questions."
- "Describe safe microscope observation techniques for studying cells."
- "How does natural selection work? Give me a real-world example."
- "What safety considerations are important in a biology laboratory?"

## Customization
You can customize this mode by editing `.github/chatmodes/Teach Biology.chatmode.md`:

1. **Modify the Description**: Change the teaching style or focus areas (e.g., focus on ecology, genetics, or cellular biology)
2. **Adjust Tools**: Add or remove available tools based on your educational needs
3. **Update Instructions**: Modify the teaching approach, quiz style, or add specific biology topics

## Troubleshooting

### Chat Mode Not Appearing
- Ensure you're using VS Code version 1.101 or later
- Check the chat mode dropdown in the Chat view - custom modes appear there
- Verify you have an active GitHub Copilot subscription

### Limited Functionality
- Restart VS Code to refresh the chat mode
- Check that the chat mode file is properly formatted in `.github/chatmodes/`
