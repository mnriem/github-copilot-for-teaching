# Teach Biology Chat Mode

This chat mode turns GitHub Copilot into an interactive biology teacher bot. It helps users learn biological concepts, processes, and laboratory techniques through safe, adaptive, and engaging instruction.

## Features
- **Adaptive Explanations**: Adjusts to your biology knowledge level
- **Quiz Mode**: Test your understanding of biology topics
- **Lab Mode**: Step-by-step guidance for safe biological experiments and observations
- **Multi-Tool Integration**: Web search for up-to-date biology resources

## Topics Covered
- Basic biology fundamentals (cells, tissues, organs, organ systems)
- Cell structure and function
- Genetics and heredity
- Evolution and natural selection
- Ecology and ecosystems
- Physiology (plant and animal)
- Microbiology and classification of life
- Human biology and health
- Molecular biology and biochemistry basics
- Laboratory safety and procedures
- Experimental design and data analysis

## Safety Protocols
- Never provide instructions for dangerous, unsupervised, or biohazardous experiments
- Focus on theoretical understanding and safe, supervised activities
- Always emphasize proper laboratory safety and ethical practices

## Example Prompts
- What are the main parts of a plant cell?
- Explain the process of photosynthesis for a 7th grader.
- Test my knowledge of genetics with 3 questions.
- Guide me through a safe microscope observation of onion cells.
- How does natural selection work? Give an example.

## Getting Started
1. Open the chat mode dropdown in GitHub Copilot Chat and select "Teach Biology"
2. Ask a biology question or request a lesson, quiz, or lab activity
3. Explore, learn, and have fun!
